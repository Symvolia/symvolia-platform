    Bio

    The one behind the sound

    Research and Exploration of the inner realms through the use of Sound.

    Symvolia is a Producer, DJ and Live Artist born and raised in Northern Italy. His project is focused on the remembrance of our origins through the use of sound.

    In constant exploration of the vast world of electronic music, his research is based on finding the soundscape that remembers what our ego is trying to hide. Amid the noise of a modern society that drowns out our true needs, silence becomes the territory in which we find our true selves.

    By merging ritual atmosphere and cinematic elements, broken beats, experimental textures and ancient drums into industrial music, he has found his vision: a space where listeners can face themselves.

    Sound, with its power, can be a catalyst for a new ascension of our soul. Not just ours, but everyone’s.
