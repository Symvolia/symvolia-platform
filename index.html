Bio

The one behind the sound

Symvolia is a Producer, DJ and Live Artist born and raised in North-Italy. His project is focused on the remembrance of our origins through the use of sounds. In constant exploration of the vast world of electronic music, his research is based on finding the soundscape that remember what our ego is trying to hide.

Between the noise of the modern society that close the listening of our true needs, the silences become the territory in which we find our true selves. If all of us can listen deeply and openly to ourselves, we are able to return to our origin.

By merging ritual atmosphere and cinematic elements, broken beats, experimental textures and ancient drums into industrial music, he has found his vision; a space where the listener can face himself.

The sound, with his power, can be a catalyst to a new ascension of our soul. Not just ours, but everyone’s.

The Echo of your Own Soul

Sound as Passage

Research and Exploration of the inner realms through the use of Sound.

Symvolia treats music as a medium for inner exploration — sculpting soundscapes that weave orchestral depth, raw noise, and the resonance of ancient drums. Guided by ritual energy and cinematic darkness, each work invites the listener to surrender the senses and descend toward the essence of the true self.
